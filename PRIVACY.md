# Rapporteur privacy policy

Rapporteur does not collect, send or share any of your data with the developer.

- Rapporteur talks only to GitHub (github.com and api.github.com), with your own GitHub account.
  It reads the list of your repositories and their labels, and it creates the issues that you write.
- Your GitHub access token stays in the Keychain of your iPhone.
- Your favourite repositories, their labels and the issues that wait in the outbox stay on your iPhone.
- Rapporteur has no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

To remove the access, sign out in the app, or revoke Rapporteur at
github.com/settings/applications.

Questions: https://github.com/knorthfield/rapporteur/issues
